# Keel: concept and direction

## Brief
- Product: a focus and time-blocking planner. Works fully, saves on the device (no accounts). Light + dark switch.
- One product page: a hero showing the planner's day live, then four feature demo sections the visitor scrolls to:
  drag a task into the day, focus timer, week review, quick add in plain words.
- Each demo plays when scrolled into view (10–15 s loop), stops out of view, has a pause button.
- "Modern, classy, premium, and high-budget."

## Concept
**Keel turns tasks into time blocks, and shows how deep your day really goes.**

Job: When I start a workday with more to do than hours, I want to give each task a place in the day and protect the
hours for hard work, so I end the day having done the important thing, not just the urgent ones.

- Who: a knowledge worker (product, design, engineering, writing) planning on a laptop at 8:30, glancing back between
  blocks; checks the week on Friday.
- Outcome: the deep-work hours they planned actually happen, and they can see it.
- Surfaces: Operate (the app), Persuade (the product page with demos).

## Five lines
| Line | |
|---|---|
| Product | A day planner where tasks become time blocks and blocks become focus sessions |
| Character | Unhurried, exact, deep |
| World | Water and its depth: the surface is meetings and email, the deep is focused work. A keel is what keeps a boat steady below the surface |
| Moments | The depth profile of the day (Today); the focus ring (Focus) |
| Signature | The depth profile: the day drawn as a sounding line across the hours, dipping deep where focused work is planned; done hours solid, planned hours hatched, a mark at now |

Idea sentence: on Keel, **the kinds of work in a day** become **a depth sounding: the deeper the line dives, the more
focused the hour**.

Template test: a generic calendar shows blocks and a now line, and any rival could ship that. Only a focus planner
sorts its hours by depth, so the sounding line belongs to Keel.

## Direction contract
| Block | Contents |
|---|---|
| Feeling | Unhurried, exact, deep |
| Direction and why | "Deep water on paper": warm paper ground, quiet chrome, one deep-water ink that carries focus. Wins because the product's own data (kinds of work) gives the signature its shape, and a single deep, rich colour on paper reads premium (taste: warmth from material and one deep object) |
| Surface | Operate (app), Persuade (product page) |
| Scene | A person at a desk at 8:30 in daylight planning the day; a dark switch for evening work → light default, dark designed |
| Type | Hanken Grotesk (UI, 400/500/600, tabular figures verified on an earlier build). Newsreader (marketing headlines and the page's section titles only, 400/500, optical sizes). App scale 12/13/14/16/20/24; marketing fluid steps |
| Colour | Ground warm paper (hue 80, chroma .006). Deep water (hue 225) = deep work and the primary action. Kind colours carry one meaning each: deep (deep water), shallow work (pale sea-glass, hue 195), meetings (coral, hue 35), breaks (sage, hue 150). The block in progress is the one deep-filled object; others are pale tints. Restrained posture: strong ink ≤ 5% outside the timeline |
| Density and grid | App: 3 columns at 1440 (tasks 300, day flexible, side panel 340), one 16 px gap; phone stacks. Page: contained 1312, sections alternate copy left / demo right |
| Radius and depth | 16 px cards, 10 px blocks and controls, 999 px pill buttons (flat, colour-only hover). Hairlines on cards; one soft neutral shadow on floating things (drag ghost, sheet, focus panel). Dark: lighter surfaces, no shadow |
| Motion | Calm and exact: blocks settle into place (220 ms out-curve), digits roll, the ring sweeps continuously in focus. Nothing loops in the app |
| Signature | The depth profile at the top of the day column; recurs small in the week review as small multiples |
| References | None used. Quality bar from the taste profile: Apple, Linear, Mercury qualities only |

## Alternates considered
1. **Morning light**: warm white and a sunlight band that crosses the day; the now line is a strip of window light. Softer, more lifestyle; weaker on data.
2. **Night shift ledger**: dark-first, graphite and a single signal amber, dense mono timings. Sharp and expert, colder than the taste profile asks for.
