Every hour has a depth

Keel draws your day as a line, like a depth reading of water. Meetings and email keep you near the surface; deep work dives. One look tells you whether today has room for the work that matters.

  • Deep workWriting, building, thinking hard. The line dives.
  • Shallow workEmail, admin, reviews. Just under the surface.
  • MeetingCalls and reviews with people. Near the top.
  • BreakLunch, a walk, a reset. At the surface.

Give every task a place

Drag a task from your list onto a free hour. Keel sizes the block from your estimate, and the depth line shows how much of the day is now set aside for real work.

  • Blocks snap to 15 minutes
  • Arrow keys move a block; Shift with arrows resizes it
  • No mouse? Schedule puts it in the next free slot

When it’s time, only the block remains

Start focus and the planner steps back: one block, one ring, the minutes left. Finish, and the time counts toward your deep work for the day.

  • Pause, add five minutes, or leave and keep your time
  • Space pauses, Escape leaves
  • Runs to the end of the block you’re in

See how deep your week went

Each day’s planned deep work against what really happened, with the shape of the day beside it. Keel names the day that went deepest and how often the plan held.

  • Hatched is planned, solid is done
  • Open any day from its row
  • Counts only time you marked done or spent in focus

Type it the way you’d say it

“Write launch email 90m tomorrow 9am” becomes a 90-minute block, tomorrow at nine. Leave out the time and it waits in your list. It’s worked out on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

  • Understands 90m, 1.5h, 1h30, noon, 3pm, Friday
  • Guesses the kind of work from the words, or use #deep, #meeting
  • Press N anywhere in the planner to start typing
